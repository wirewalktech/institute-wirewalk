Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This page explains what the Wirewalk Institute of AI (operated by Wirewalk) collects when you use institute.wirewalk.com and its learner area, and why. Questions or requests: sales@wirewalk.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, optional organisation, and a password stored only as a salted hash.
  • Learning records: enrolments, lessons completed, exam attempts and scores, and certificates issued.
  • Purchases: what you bought, the amount and its status. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never receive your card number.
  • Enquiries and waitlist sign-ups: what you send through the teams form or a waitlist form — name, email, organisation, team size, the course you asked about and your message. Each submission is stored with the IP address and browser user-agent it came from, which we use to spot and block abuse.
  • Security records: IP addresses and timestamps used to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts, and the IP address and user-agent recorded with each enquiry as described above.
  • Campaign source: if you arrive through a tagged link (for example one of our ads on Reddit), the link's campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), Reddit's click ID (rdt_cid, if the link carries one) and the page you landed on are saved with your account when you sign up, so we can tell which campaigns lead to sign-ups. If you then send the teams enquiry form, the same kind of arrival details go with that enquiry (see In your browser). Our servers do not send them to Reddit or anyone else.{% if rdt_on != "" %} If you accept advertising cookies, the Reddit Pixel itself reads Reddit's click ID from the link (see Advertising cookies).{% endif %}
  • Page and button counts: on our campaign pages we count page views and clicks on the main buttons as anonymous daily totals (a page or button name and a number per day). No IP address, cookie or user ID is stored with those counts.

What is public

A certificate's verification page is public by design. It shows the name on the certificate, the course or program, the issue date, the exam score and whether it is valid. It does not show your email address or any other account details.

What we use it for

To run your account and courses, grade exams, issue and verify certificates, process purchases, answer enquiries, send the emails the service needs (such as sign-in codes and certificates), and protect the service from abuse. We do not sell personal data.{% if rdt_on != "" %} The only third-party advertising tool on this site is the Reddit Pixel, and it runs only if you accept it (see Advertising cookies). There are no analytics scripts.{% else %} This site uses no third-party advertising or analytics trackers: no ad pixels, no analytics scripts.{% endif %}

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this public website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface used for headings, so your browser requests it from Google.

In your browser

We store a sign-in token in your browser's local storage while you are signed in, your light or dark theme choice, a short-lived copy of the price list, and, only if you arrived through a tagged campaign link, those campaign tags for up to 30 days so they can go with your sign-up (the browser copy is deleted when you create an account, or when it expires). Separately, for enquiries, your browser keeps for up to 30 days how you first and most recently reached this site: campaign tags and any ad click ID in the link (utm_*, gclid, gbraid, wbraid, msclkid, rdt_cid, li_fat_id, fbclid), the page you landed on (without its query string) and the name of the referring website (not its full address). It is sent only with a teams enquiry you submit, never on its own and never to a third party. {% if rdt_on != "" %}We also store your cookie choice (accept or reject) in local storage, and, if you accept, a short list of purchase and sign-up references already reported to Reddit so none is reported twice. Our own site sets no tracking cookies; the Reddit Pixel sets cookies only after you accept, as described next.{% else %}There are no tracking cookies.{% endif %}

Advertising cookies (Reddit Pixel)

We advertise on Reddit and would like to measure which ads lead to visits, sign-ups and purchases. For that we use the Reddit Pixel, a script from Reddit. On your first visit a banner asks you to Accept or Reject it; both choices are equally easy, and nothing from Reddit loads until you press Accept. If you reject, or ignore the banner, the pixel never loads.

If you accept, your browser loads the script from www.redditstatic.com and sends events to Reddit (alb.reddit.com). Reddit receives what any request to it carries (your IP address, browser details and the address of the page), and the script sets Reddit cookies on this site (named _rdt_…), including Reddit's click ID if you arrived from a Reddit ad. We report these events:

  • PageVisit for each page you view;
  • SignUp when you create an account, with our internal account reference;
  • Lead when you enrol in a free course;
  • Purchase when a paid order is confirmed, with our order reference, the amount, the currency and the number of courses.

We do not give the pixel your name, email address or any other account details. When you sign up after accepting, we also record on your account that you accepted, and update that record if you later change your choice while signed in. Our servers do not send any of your data to Reddit.

You can change your choice at any time: use Cookie settings in the footer of any page, or the button below. Rejecting after you had accepted deletes the Reddit cookies on this site and reloads the page without the pixel.

How long we keep it

Learning records and certificates are kept while your account exists so certificates stay verifiable. Purchase records are kept as long as accounting law requires. Ask us to close your account and we will delete or anonymise what we are not required to keep; a certificate you have shared can be withdrawn from public verification on request.

Your choices

You can update your name and organisation in your account. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted, email sales@wirewalk.com from the address on your account.

Verify a certificate

Enter the ID printed at the top right of the certificate. The result comes straight from our register at the moment you check.

A valid result confirms the certificate was issued by the Wirewalk Institute of AI, has not been revoked and, if it carries an expiry date, has not expired. Read what a certificate attests and what it does not before relying on it.

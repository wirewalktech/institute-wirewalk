Pricing

One price per course. No subscription.

You pay once for a course, and its lessons, exam attempts and certificate are included. Prices are set by level and shown live from our billing system, so what you see here is what checkout charges before any code or offer.

Free Intro courses are free: about an hour each, with the same exam and verifiable certificate. Sign in and enroll, no card needed. See the free courses.

Bundles

Three ways to buy more for less

Build your own bundle

Put several paid courses in your cart and each one costs less.

Free courses, courses you already own, programs, track bundles and the pass do not count toward it. Your cart tells you how many more courses reach the next step.

Choose courses →

Buy a whole track

Every paid course on sale in a track, at a bundle price shown on each track page. If you are signed in, courses you already own are left out.

A track bundle includes the courses on sale on the day you buy it. Courses added to the track later are not included.

See the tracks →

All-Access Pass

Every course on sale for days, including courses we launch while your pass lasts. One payment; it does not renew by itself.

Get the pass →

Every course on sale for the length of the pass, including courses we launch while your pass lasts. One payment; it does not renew by itself. The price is shown at checkout.

Certificates you earn stay valid after the pass ends; lessons and exams of courses you did not buy separately lock until you renew. Buying again while a pass is active adds a full pass after it ends. The pass is for one person; for teams, contact sales, or see organization pricing.

Offers never stack. On each item you get the single best offer you qualify for, whether that is a bundle saving, a sale, a code or a personal offer, and no item is ever more than 70% off. Sales and codes apply to the pass only when they say so.

Ways to save

Programs, sales and codes

Certification programs

Four courses for a role, bought together for less than separately. Each program page shows the bundle price against the separate total. See programs.

Sales with an end date

When a course is on sale, its page shows the sale price, the regular price struck through, and a countdown to the moment the sale ends. No fake timers: the end date is the one checkout uses.

Codes and personal offers

Enter a discount code in your cart. If an offer applies to you or your organization, it appears in the cart once you are signed in. The single best offer is applied to each item (a program, track or build-your-own bundle saving counts as an offer), and offers never stack.

Paying

Checkout is handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. Your enrollment starts as soon as Stripe confirms the payment.

Organizations that need a quote, an invoice or a purchase order can arrange that with us instead of paying by card. Pricing agreed for a whole organization, with invoices on agreed terms, starts with the organization inquiry.

What every course includes

  • All lessons in the course, self-paced
  • A timed exam, with up to three attempts
  • A certificate of completion and assessment with a public verification page
  • Module-level feedback after each attempt

Prices are in US dollars.

Transparency

Standards and compliance

We publish verified standards coverage only when supported by evidence. Courses not yet assessed are Under Review.

Current status: Under Review

All courses without a completed, documented standards assessment are classified Under Review for ISO alignment. This does not mean the course fails an ISO requirement; it means conformity has not been established. Enrollment availability and educational completion status are separate from ISO review status.

Wirewalk Institute of AI does not currently claim ISO certification, ISO accreditation, or ISO endorsement. Our existing course certificates attest to lesson completion and performance on an online, unproctored assessment, not independently verified professional competence. Read certificate limitations.

Standards we evaluate

  • ISO 29993:2017: requirements for learning services outside formal education. This is our primary candidate for course-delivery alignment.
  • ISO 21001:2025: institute-wide educational organization management systems; not a certification of an individual course.
  • ISO/IEC 17024:2026: requirements for bodies certifying persons. Course completion is not equivalent to accredited personnel certification.
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023: AI management systems. A course may teach this standard without the institute or learner being certified to it.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022: information security management systems. Curriculum coverage is distinct from organizational conformity.

Our review and remediation process

  1. Assess

    Map the applicable standard and edition to objectives, lesson materials, exercises, assessments, learner support and documented delivery processes.

  2. Record gaps

    Identify missing evidence and nonconformities. Preserve an audit trail, including reviewer, date, standard scope and source evidence.

  3. Revise and retest

    Update course material or assessment methods, validate the revisions, and re-review against the same requirements.

  4. Publish the supported status

    Only after documented review may a course show Curriculum Mapped or Alignment Verified. Full conformity claims require an assessment of all applicable requirements, including institute-level processes.

Status definitions: Under Review = not yet fully assessed; Remediation Required = assessed gaps exist; Curriculum Mapped = topic coverage documented; Alignment Verified = reviewed evidence supports a specifically defined alignment scope. None of these statuses means ISO-accredited.

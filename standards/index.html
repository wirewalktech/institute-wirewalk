Transparency

Standards and alignment

Our curriculum mappings identify specific educational coverage. They are separate from organizational conformity, third-party certification and demonstrated workplace competence.

Mapping review: October 19, 2026.

Published mapping scopes

Curriculum Mapped means topic correspondence has been documented and reviewed for the named course and edition. It does not approve every source, exercise, practical skill or framework requirement. Courses without a documented mapping remain Under Review for that framework. No professional-body credits or qualifications are assigned by these mappings.

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2026 edition): Curriculum Mapped for sec-201. See mapping and assessment limits. Official 2026 resource.
  • NIST AI RMF 1.0 — NIST AI 100-1: Curriculum Mapped (gov-201): see mapping. Official publication, DOI 10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1.
  • NIST AI 600-1 — Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile: Curriculum Mapped (gov-201): see mapping and gaps. Official profile.
  • Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity (NICE Framework), NIST SP 800-181 Rev. 1: mapping status Under Review; no course or work-role competency mapping is claimed here. Official framework. Its separately versioned components are listed as v2.2.0 (April 28, 2025) on the current-versions page.

gov-201: NIST curriculum mapping

AI Governance and Risk Management maps selected teaching and assessment evidence to AI RMF 1.0 and its Generative AI Profile. This is a course-topic mapping, not NIST certification, endorsement or an organizational maturity assessment.

  • GOVERN and MAP: governance, accountability, context and proceed/hold decisions are taught and sampled in the item bank. GOVERN assessment is thin; this does not establish coverage of every category or subcategory.
  • MEASURE and MANAGE: selected teaching and item-bank evidence address deployment-context evaluation, risks that cannot be measured reliably, treatment and deactivation.
  • Profiles and generative AI risks: teaching covers profile comparison and the AI 600-1 risk taxonomy. The selected assessment recognizes some risks; it does not establish that all twelve profile risks or their suggested actions are assessed.

The reviewed course mappings do not establish full course, practical or framework-conformity approval. A reference to a framework is not an endorsement by its publisher.

ISO review status: Under Review

All courses without a completed, documented ISO standards assessment are Under Review for ISO alignment. This does not mean a course fails an ISO requirement; conformity has not been established. Enrollment availability, curriculum mapping and educational completion status are separate from ISO review status.

Wirewalk Institute of AI does not currently claim ISO certification, ISO accreditation or ISO endorsement. Course certificates attest to lesson completion and performance on an online, unproctored assessment, not independently verified professional competence.

Standards we evaluate

  • ISO 29993:2017: requirements for learning services outside formal education. This is our primary candidate for course-delivery alignment.
  • ISO 21001:2025: institute-wide educational organization management systems; not a certification of an individual course.
  • ISO/IEC 17024:2026: requirements for bodies certifying persons. Course completion is not equivalent to accredited personnel certification.
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023: AI management systems. A course may teach this standard without the institute or learner being certified to it.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022: information security management systems. Curriculum coverage is distinct from organizational conformity.

Our review and remediation process

  1. Assess

    Map the applicable standard and edition to objectives, lesson materials, exercises, assessments, learner support and documented delivery processes.

  2. Record gaps

    Identify missing evidence and nonconformities. Preserve an audit trail, including reviewer, date, standard scope and source evidence.

  3. Revise and retest

    Update course material or assessment methods, validate the revisions, and re-review against the same requirements.

  4. Publish the supported status

    Only after documented review may a course show Curriculum Mapped or Alignment Verified. Full conformity claims require an assessment of all applicable requirements, including institute-level processes.

Status definitions: Under Review = not yet fully assessed for the stated scope; Remediation Required = assessed gaps exist; Curriculum Mapped = topic coverage documented; Alignment Verified = reviewed evidence supports a specifically defined alignment scope. None of these statuses means ISO-accredited.

Courses

What is a course like?

Written lessons grouped into five or six modules, with worked examples and hands-on exercises you can try with the tools the course covers. You work at your own pace and your progress is saved as you go. Each course page lists its modules and lesson titles before you buy.

Which level should I start at?

Basic assumes no prior experience. Intermediate is for people who already use or build with AI. Expert is deep technical work for specialists. Executive is for leaders, boards and risk owners. Every course page names its prerequisites and links to the courses that cover them.

What does "Waitlist" mean?

The syllabus is published, but the lessons and exam are still being written or reviewed. A course goes on sale only after it passes review. Leave your email in the waitlist form on the course page and we will email you when it opens. Nothing is charged until then.

Do I need to buy software or accounts to take a course?

Some hands-on courses use a vendor's product or cloud account, and say so under "Before you start" on the course page. Many Basic courses need nothing more than a web browser; those built around one product, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot or Gemini, name the account you need.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA or other vendors?

No. The Wirewalk Institute of AI is independent. Courses teach publicly documented products from many vendors; no vendor has endorsed, sponsored or reviewed them.

Exams and certificates

When can I take the exam?

As soon as you have completed every lesson in the course. Then you have up to three attempts, with 24 hours between a failed attempt and the next.

What happens if I fail?

You see your score for each module so you know what to revise, and you can try again after 24 hours. Each attempt draws a fresh set of questions with the options reshuffled.

Can I see the correct answers afterwards?

No. The question bank is reused, so publishing answers would undermine every certificate. Module-level feedback points you to what to revise.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It is a certificate of completion and assessment from an independent institute: it shows you completed the course and passed its exam. It is not an academic credit, a licence, or a vendor certification. What it attests and what it does not.

How does an employer check my certificate?

Every certificate has a unique ID and a link to its verification page. Anyone can enter the ID at institute.wirewalk.com/verify to see the name, course, date, score and whether it is valid.

What name appears on my certificate?

The name on your learner account at the moment you pass. Check it in your account settings before you sit the exam.

Pricing and payment

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once per course or program. See pricing.

How do discount codes and sales work?

Sales show on the course page with the end date. Codes are entered in your cart. Only the single best offer applies to each item; offers do not stack.

Can my employer pay by invoice?

Yes. Use the teams form to ask for a quote, an invoice, or a purchase-order arrangement.

What if something goes wrong with a purchase?

Email sales@wirewalk.com with the email address you used. If a payment went through but the course did not appear, signing in again usually completes the enrolment; if it does not, we will fix it.

Accounts

Can I share my account?

No. An account, its exam attempts and its certificates belong to one person. Certificates obtained through a shared account may be revoked.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. We email a six-digit code that resets it.

Errata

Found an error? Tell us.

AI products change quickly, and we would rather hear about a mistake than leave it in a course. Every lesson and every course page has a “Report an error” link that opens this form.

What to report

  • A fact that is wrong or out of date: a product name, a limit, a setting, a date, a link.
  • An instruction that no longer works as written.
  • An exam question you believe is wrong, ambiguous, or about something the course does not teach. Describe it in your own words; please do not post the answers.
  • Typos, broken formatting, or anything that makes a lesson hard to follow.

What happens next

  1. New. Your report goes into our errata queue with the course, and the lesson if you sent it from one.
  2. Triaged. Someone on the team checks it against the course and the current vendor documentation.
  3. Fixed or won’t fix. If the course needs correcting, we correct the lesson or question. If we decide it is correct as written, we record why.

Each course page shows the month its content was last reviewed. Exam answers are never published, whatever the outcome; an administrator can review your exam attempts if you think a faulty question affected your result — email sales@wirewalk.com.

If you are signed in, the report is linked to your learner account. If not, you may leave an email address so we can ask you for details; it is optional and used only for this report. See our privacy policy.

How it works

Study, sit the exam, get a certificate anyone can check

The same four steps for every course, at every level.

  1. Work through the lessons

    Written lessons with worked examples and hands-on exercises, at your own pace. Your progress is saved as you go.

  2. Unlock the exam

    When every lesson is complete, a timed exam opens: questions drawn from a larger bank across every module, options shuffled.

  3. Pass at 80%

    Scored automatically the moment you submit. Fall short and you see how you did per module, then retry after 24 hours.

  4. Share a verifiable certificate

    A certificate with a unique ID and a public verification page. Add it to LinkedIn in one click.

The certificate

Honest about what it proves

A Wirewalk certificate states that the named person completed every lesson of a course and passed its timed exam, with the score and the date. Employers can confirm it in seconds at institute.wirewalk.com/verify.

It is a certificate of completion and assessment from an independent institute. It is not an accredited qualification, a license, or a certification from any AI vendor — and we say so on every course page.

Teams and enterprise

Training for your organization

For organizations training many people: pricing agreed with your organization, payment by invoice or purchase order on agreed terms, and training campaigns that assign courses with due dates and report progress. Tell us what you need and we will reply with what we can offer.

Organization pricing and training campaigns are being switched on now. When we reply, we tell you which parts are available for your team today.

Tell us about your organization → How team seats work

Organization pricing agreements

Pricing is agreed with each organization; there is no public discount table. An agreement can cover the team seats your managers buy, the courses your employees buy for themselves, or both, for the courses, tracks or programs you agree, until an end date. Employee pricing applies to people in your team or signed in with a verified address at an email domain your organization has proved it controls by DNS. Personal email addresses do not qualify through the domain check.

Invoice and purchase order

Without an agreement, team seats can be invoiced from 25 seats. An agreement can allow invoices for smaller orders, with payment terms agreed with you and your purchase order number on the invoice. Seats appear in your team once the invoice is paid.

Training campaigns

Your team managers assign a program or a set of courses to a list of people, with a start date and a due date. Seats come from your team's seats; if there are not enough, a manager can ask for an invoice for the rest, and nothing is charged without that request. People get email reminders before the due date, and managers see a progress report for the assigned courses only.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your organization. The form below asks for the number of learners, their roles, the topics, your timeline and how you pay. It asks for a conversation or a quote; it does not place an order.
  2. We reply. We suggest courses or programs for the roles you describe and, where it fits, propose an agreement: which purchases it covers, the pricing, the end date and the payment terms. A written quote can follow.
  3. Your team is set up. You create your team on your team page and verify your company domain with a DNS TXT record. We record the agreement for that team; an organization has one agreement in force at a time.
  4. Prices apply at checkout. The agreement is one more offer at checkout. As for any order, the single best offer applies to each item and offers never add up: if a volume tier or a sale is better than the agreement, that price applies.
  5. Assign training and follow it. Managers create training campaigns, and people get their seats, their invitation if they are not in the team yet, and reminders. The progress report shows lessons, exam results and certificates for the assigned courses. For a course someone already had before it was assigned, their progress is shown only if they choose to share it.

What we do not provide

Courses are taken here, on institute.wirewalk.com. We do not provide SCORM or xAPI packages, integration with your LMS, or single sign-on (SAML/SSO); each person signs in with their own account. If one of these is a requirement, tell us in the form below so we can say honestly whether we can help.

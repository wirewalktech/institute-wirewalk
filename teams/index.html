Teams and enterprise

Train a team. Pay the way your organization pays.

Buy seats in any course or certification program for your team, give them to people, and follow their progress, exam results and certificates in one place. Pay by card, or by invoice for larger orders.

Set up your team → Ask us first

Seats for a group

On any course or program page, choose “Buy for your team” and the number of seats, from 5 to 1,000. Volume pricing applies automatically and the checkout shows the exact price before you pay. Each person gets their own account, progress, exam attempts and certificate.

Card or invoice

Pay by card at checkout, or for 25 seats or more choose “Invoice / purchase order”: the order is recorded, we send the invoice, and the seats appear in your team once it is paid. For a written quote first, use the form below.

Progress you can report on

Your team page shows, for each person, the courses their seat gave them, lessons completed, exam status and best score, and certificates, with a CSV download. You see only the courses your team gave them, not anything else they study.

How team seats work

  1. Create a team. Sign in, verify your email address, and create the team on your team page. You are its owner; you can make other members managers.
  2. Invite people. Paste email addresses or upload a CSV. Each person gets an email with a join link and signs in (or creates an account) with that address to join.
  3. Or let your colleagues join by domain. Verify your company domain by adding a DNS TXT record we show you. Anyone who signs in with a verified address at that domain can then join the team themselves.
  4. Buy seats and give them out. A seat gives one person a course, or every course in a program. You can release a seat and give it to someone else; the person who had it loses access to those courses, and certificates they already earned stay valid.
  5. Follow progress. Lessons, exam results and certificates per person, on screen or as a CSV.

A full refund (or a payment dispute) of a seat purchase removes the seats bought with it from everyone they were given to. As with an individual purchase, access to those courses ends and any certificate that depended on them is revoked, unless the person also holds the course another way. A partial refund leaves the seats in place; ask us if the seat count should change with it.

What we do not provide

Courses are taken here, on institute.wirewalk.com. We do not provide SCORM or xAPI packages, integration with your LMS, or single sign-on (SAML/SSO); each person signs in with their own account. If one of these is a requirement, tell us in the form below so we can say honestly whether we can help.

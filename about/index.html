About

Serious AI education, without the hype

The Wirewalk Institute of AI is the training arm of Wirewalk. It exists because most people now work alongside AI systems that nobody ever properly explained to them.

What we teach

The whole stack, in one catalog: what AI is and how to use it well; the major vendors' platforms side by side; building applications and agents; developing, training and serving models; the GPUs, networks, storage and power underneath; securing AI systems and using AI for defence; and the governance and leadership decisions on top. {{ site.data.catalog.courses.size }} courses across {{ site.data.catalog.tracks.size }} tracks, at four levels.

How we build courses

Every course starts from a published syllabus: outcomes, audience, prerequisites, modules and lesson titles. Lessons are checked against the vendors' and standards bodies' own documentation, and each course records the month its facts were last reviewed. A course goes on sale only after its lessons and its exam have passed review; until then it takes waitlist sign-ups.

AI products change monthly. When a product or rule changes materially, the affected lessons and exam questions are revised.

How we assess

Each course ends in a timed exam drawn from a larger question bank and scored automatically. We say clearly what the resulting certificate does and does not show — read how certification works.

Independence

We are not a reseller, partner or training provider for any AI vendor, and no vendor sponsors or reviews our courses. That lets a course compare products honestly and say where each one falls short.

Part of Wirewalk

Certification

An exam you have to earn, and a certificate that says exactly what it means

Every course ends in a timed, automatically scored exam. Pass it and you receive a certificate of completion and assessment that anyone can check against our public register.

Assessment

How the exam works

  1. Earned access

    The exam unlocks only after you have completed every lesson in the course.

  2. A fresh paper

    Questions are drawn from a larger bank, spread across every module, and the answer options are shuffled for each attempt.

  3. Timed and scored

    The clock runs on our server. Answers are scored automatically on submission, and the pass mark is shown on each course page — 80% for every current course.

  4. Retakes with a pause

    Miss the mark and you can try again after 24 hours, up to three attempts per course.

The rules, in full

  • Question count and time limit are set per course and listed on its page: typically 20 questions in 40–45 minutes at Basic and Intermediate, 25 in 50–60 at Expert, and 15 in 30 at Executive.
  • Questions with several correct answers score only when you select exactly the right set — no part marks.
  • The deadline is firm. A short grace period covers network delay; a paper submitted later than that is scored as unanswered.
  • Interrupted? Opening the exam again resumes your open attempt with the same clock rather than starting a new one.
  • Feedback after each attempt shows your score per module, so you know what to revise. We never release the answer key.

Why no answer key?

The bank is reused across attempts and learners. Publishing answers would make every later certificate worth less — including yours. Module-level feedback tells you where to look; the lessons have the rest.

The certificate

What it attests — and what it does not

It attests that

  • The account holder completed every lesson of the named course.
  • They passed its timed exam, with the score and date shown.
  • The certificate was issued by the Wirewalk Institute of AI and has not been revoked, as the verification page confirms.

It does not attest that

  • The holder's identity was checked. The name is the one on the learner's account when they passed.
  • The exam was proctored. It is taken online, unsupervised.
  • The holder is competent in a workplace. A multiple-choice exam samples knowledge; it does not observe practice.
  • Any vendor recognises it. It is not a certification from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA or anyone else.
  • It is accredited. It is not an academic credit, degree, licence or regulated qualification.

We may revoke a certificate issued in error or obtained by misconduct. A revoked certificate shows as revoked on its verification page.

Sample

What you receive

A landscape certificate you can print or save as a PDF, showing your name, the course, the issue date, your exam score and a unique certificate ID. It links to its own verification page, and its page in your learner account has a one-click “Add to LinkedIn” link.

Complete every course in a certification program and a program certificate is issued automatically, with its own ID.

The name, ID, course and score shown are placeholders.

Verification

Anyone can check a certificate in seconds

Enter the certificate ID, or follow the link printed on it. The page shows the holder's name, the course or program, the issue date, the score, and whether the certificate is valid or revoked. It shows nothing else — no email address, no account details.
